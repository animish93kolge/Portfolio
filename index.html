The Editor

About

I turn complex technology into simple, intuitive experiences people genuinely enjoy using.

My role goes beyond interface design — I work across product strategy, user experience, design systems, AI experiences and cross-functional leadership. I enjoy solving ambiguous problems, aligning user needs with business goals, and building systems that let teams move faster without compromising quality.

AI is woven into how I work — accelerating research synthesis, uncovering insights, exploring multiple directions, catching edge cases and streamlining collaboration with product and engineering. It doesn't replace design thinking; it buys me more time for the strategic calls and the meaningful user problems.

I believe great products are built at the intersection of user empathy, business impact and technical feasibility — whether I'm defining product vision, mentoring designers, establishing process, or crafting the smallest interaction.

How I lead design

People · Business · Cost · Process
People

Build teams that grow.

I own hiring, mentorship and performance for my team, with a KRA/KPI framework tied to company OKRs and each designer's career growth. I also helped set up and lead the Infosys Design Studio.

Business

Design that moves numbers.

I tie design to business goals. At PayNearby, daily train and flight bookings rose 12%, and the gold-loan and mutual-fund journey went from 18 steps to 12.

Cost

More output, leaner team.

My team absorbed a workload that would normally need two more junior designers, through better work planning and AI — and I manage AI token usage for maximum output at minimum cost.

Process

Systems that scale quality.

I set up design governance, a design operations model and a design maturity model with regular audits, cutting handoff friction and rework between design and engineering.
